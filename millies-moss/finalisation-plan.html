Finalisation & Optimisation Plan

The main rebuild sprint is complete. This phase is about refinement, cleanup, optimisation and launch hardening — not reopening the core design.

Objective

Finish the rebuilt store to production standard.

Complete the remaining content, catalogue, backend, app, SEO, accessibility, analytics and QA work so the site is genuinely finished and maintainable.

Working rule: only change core architecture or design if testing exposes a real defect or material usability problem.

Workstream 1

Catalogue foundation

Clean the underlying store structure before polishing content.

01Product content overhaulP0Make every PDP complete, consistent, evidence-led and conversion-ready.+
What needs doing

Standardise titles, descriptions, ingredients, directions, storage, FAQs, SEO, alt text and related products. Add evidence-supported reasons to buy and review health/wellness wording for compliance.

Done when
Every live PDP follows one agreed structure and passes factual, claims, spelling, SEO and conversion review.
02Product catalogue cleanupP0Remove catalogue debt and stop internal products surfacing to customers.+
What needs doing

Audit active, draft and archived products; separate customer products from operational add-ons; review duplicate/legacy records, SKUs, tags, product types, metafields, channels and collection membership.

Done when
Only intentional products are discoverable by customers; operational records remain safely hidden.
03Product imagery standardisationP0Create one visual system for every product card and PDP gallery.+
What needs doing

Standardise aspect ratio, crop, background, product scale and image sequence. Optimise responsive sizing, compression, modern formats and alt text.

Done when
Product cards and PDP galleries look intentionally consistent across mobile and desktop.
04Collection architecture cleanupP0Consolidate duplicate and legacy collections without breaking feeds or URLs.+
What needs doing

Classify every collection as Keep / Merge / Hide / Redirect / Delete. Check rules, memberships, sorting, app/feed dependencies and navigation destinations.

Done when
Collections support the new IA cleanly with no unnecessary duplicates or broken dependencies.
26Full Shopify backend hygieneP0Complete a full Shopify admin clean-up so products, collections, files and app-created records are intentional and current.+
What needs doing

Audit the full Files library for unused media; review 27 archived products, 23 draft products and all 48 collections; clean obsolete tags, product types, vendors, metafields/metaobjects, menus, redirects and app-created records. Verify references and dependencies before deletion.

Done when
Shopify contains only intentional products, collections, media and supporting data; unused assets are removed safely and no live content or dependency is broken.
Workstream 2

Content & brand polish

Bring every customer-facing page up to the rebuilt standard.

05Support & help centre refinementP0Turn support content into a clear, polished self-service experience.+
What needs doing

Refine shipping, returns, storage, FAQs, subscriptions, accounts, referrals, gift cards and contact content. Improve layouts and fix known sidebar, form, gift-card and referral issues.

Done when
Support pages are complete, consistent and every navigation/form path works.
06Journal, articles & recipesP0Bring the editorial experience up to the standard of the new storefront.+
What needs doing

Rewrite legacy copy, remove unsupported claims, improve imagery/internal links, refine Journal/article/recipe templates, fix sidebar/back/related navigation and improve SEO/schema/AEO.

Done when
Every article and recipe uses the refined template, reviewed copy and appropriate imagery.
07Static, educational & policy contentP0Make every non-commerce page feel part of the rebuilt brand.+
What needs doing

Review Our Story, Journal landing, educational and policy pages; align copy, layout and imagery; fix stale destinations such as Sea Moss Explained.

Done when
All informational pages are current, coherent and visually integrated with the rebuilt site.
23Customer email notificationsP1Make post-purchase communication feel like the same brand.+
What needs doing

Review order, shipping, delivery, refund/cancellation, account, gift-card, subscription and contact acknowledgement emails.

Done when
Transactional messages are accurate, branded and free of legacy wording.
Workstream 3

Apps & platform

Remove technical debt and make the Shopify setup intentional.

08Complete app audit + capability-gap reviewP0Reduce app debt, optimise what remains and identify worthwhile missing capabilities.+
What needs doing

Audit every installed app for purpose, cost, usage, theme impact and data ownership. Remove redundant app residue. Fully optimise retained apps. Then review capability gaps such as subscriptions/retention, reviews/UGC, referrals/loyalty, bundles/upsells, search/filtering, support, email/SMS, analytics/attribution, accessibility and operations. Only adopt new apps with a documented business case.

Done when
The stack is lean and fully configured, and any recommended new app has a justified benefit that outweighs cost, complexity and storefront weight.
17Shopify configuration auditP1Confirm the platform configuration matches the intended commerce model.+
What needs doing

Review Markets, currencies, shipping, taxes, payments, accounts, checkout, inventory locations, gift cards, discounts, policies, domains and sender authentication.

Done when
Core Shopify settings are intentional and free from conflicting legacy configuration.
18Inventory & SKU integrityP1Remove preventable stock and fulfilment risks.+
What needs doing

Audit SKU uniqueness, missing SKUs, inventory tracking, overselling, negative inventory on add-ons and bundle inventory behaviour.

Done when
Inventory and SKU behaviour is predictable across customer-facing products and operational add-ons.
19Analytics & tracking validationP1Make conversion reporting trustworthy.+
What needs doing

Verify Shopify analytics, GA4 and paid-media pixels/APIs; review consent; validate core funnel events and duplicate purchase tracking.

Done when
Core funnel events fire once, consent is respected and reporting is reliable.
Workstream 4

Quality assurance

Prove the experience works across journeys, devices and edge states.

09Customer journey functional QAP0Prove the complete buying journey works end to end.+
What needs doing

Test Homepage → Navigation/Search → Collection → PDP → Cart → Checkout → Confirmation → Account. Cover variants, bundles, discounts, subscriptions, upsells, stock states, delivery, gift cards, forms, search, filters, reviews and referrals.

Done when
Critical journeys succeed without dead ends, misleading states or blocking defects.
10Automated QA suiteP0Create repeatable automated checks across key templates.+
What needs doing

Run Lighthouse Performance, Accessibility, SEO and Best Practices, Core Web Vitals, broken links, 404s, console/network errors, media/alt checks, structured data and responsive screenshots.

Done when
Automated issues are triaged and all launch-blocking defects are closed.
11Responsive & browser QAP0Validate the experience across real viewport and browser combinations.+
What needs doing

Review mobile, tablet, laptop, desktop and wide desktop. Test Safari iOS, Chrome Android, Safari/Chrome desktop, Firefox and Edge.

Done when
No critical layout, interaction or navigation defects remain in supported environments.
12Accessibility auditP0Check usability beyond Lighthouse scores.+
What needs doing

Test keyboard navigation, focus, headings, forms, modals/drawers, contrast, accessible names, product controls, search, accordions, reduced motion and touch targets.

Done when
Core templates and journeys are usable by keyboard and meet the agreed accessibility baseline.
24Error & edge statesP1Make failure states understandable instead of surprising.+
What needs doing

Test 404, empty states, sold out, invalid discount, payment failure, form errors, app-unavailable states, image failure, long titles and missing product media.

Done when
Common error states explain what happened and give customers a useful next action.
Workstream 5

Discoverability & UX

Finish SEO, search, navigation and conversion refinement.

13SEO & discoverability crawlP1Remove indexing and discoverability debt before final sign-off.+

Done when
No unresolved high-impact indexing, duplication, broken-link or structured-data issues remain.

15Search qualityP1Make on-site search useful for real customer intent.+

Done when
Core customer searches return relevant products/content and useful no-result states.
16Navigation & internal-link auditP1Catch stale destinations left by the rebuild.+
What needs doing

Check header, mobile nav, footer, CTAs, breadcrumbs, support navigation, Journal navigation, PDP links, cross-sells and article links.

Done when
Primary and secondary navigation paths are correct and consistent.
20PDP conversion reviewP1Refine purchase clarity after the content pass.+
What needs doing

Re-check price/variant clarity, subscriptions, delivery messaging, trust, reviews, add-to-cart prominence, benefits, FAQs and related products.

Done when
Representative PDPs communicate value and purchase choices clearly.
21Cart & checkout polishP1Remove final friction in the purchase flow.+
What needs doing

Test cart drawer, empty cart, upsells, protection, insulated packaging, priority handling, free-shipping messaging, subscriptions, discounts, errors and checkout branding.

Done when
Cart and checkout are clear, stable and free from conflicting add-ons or messaging.
22Account area final passP1Close the remaining account-area polish.+
What needs doing

Re-check card height/layout consistency and test orders, addresses, login/logout and responsive states.

Done when
Account pages remain consistent and functional after all refinements.
Workstream 6

Technical closeout

Clean the codebase and freeze a maintainable final release.

25Final theme/code cleanupP1Leave a clean, maintainable production theme.+
What needs doing

Remove obsolete sections/snippets, dead CSS/JS, unused assets and safe-to-remove app remnants. Review console/network activity, font/image loading, rerun Lighthouse and tag the final release in GitHub.

Done when
The production theme is clean, versioned, regression-tested and ready for ongoing maintenance.
Definition of done

Implemented, checked, accepted.

The rebuild is complete only when the remaining work is implemented and verified — not simply documented. Final sign-off should cover visual review, mobile and desktop checks, functional behaviour, performance, accessibility, content accuracy, edge cases, analytics, SEO and acceptance by Tashan or Leanne.
